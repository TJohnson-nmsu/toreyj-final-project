GreenTech Rooftop Garden Challenge

Welcome to the GreenTech Rooftop Garden Challenge! In this interactive game, you will take on the role of a rooftop gardener tasked with creating a thriving green space in an urban environment. Your goal is to design and maintain a sustainable rooftop garden that not only beautifies the cityscape but also contributes to environmental health and community well-being.

As part of Aloe's commitment to sustainability, we are excited to offer this unique opportunity to engage with our green technology and make a positive impact on your local environment.
